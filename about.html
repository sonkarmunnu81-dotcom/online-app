About CareerPath

Online Career Guidance Portal for Underprivileged Students

What is this portal?

CareerPath is a student-friendly frontend project that brings career exploration, assessment, education pathways, scholarships and learning resources together.

Why was it created?

Students may struggle to access reliable career information, understand eligibility and discover affordable learning opportunities.

Objectives

Reduce information barriers, encourage informed decisions and connect learners with trustworthy official resources.

Benefits

Simple navigation, career cards, roadmaps, assessment guidance, saved items and responsive access.

Future Scope

Secure authentication, verified live scholarship APIs, professional counselling, multilingual content and institutional partnerships.

Mission

“To make reliable career information and learning opportunities accessible to students from every financial background.”

Vision

“Every student should have the opportunity to understand their potential and make an informed career decision.”
